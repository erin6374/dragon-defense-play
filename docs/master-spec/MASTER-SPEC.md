## Product experience
Paulo, a heroic dragon mascot, defends the recognizable Tech Freire school (2221 N Broad Street, Philadelphia) and neighborhood against escalating meteor bombardment. Missile Command-inspired action in a genuine 3D city with an angled, tracking 2.5D gameplay camera. Cinematic disaster, not generic flat city or toy voxel blocks. Foreground gameplay is readable and responsive; the skyline reflects an escalating parallel catastrophe. Players protect school/community, score interceptions, earn powers and choose finale outcomes. Make controls discoverable and opening approachable. No actual campaign pause or freeze except intentional pause and cutscene; respawn only at chapter starts/cutscenes as requested, collision bumps must not steal control indefinitely. Training is a safe separate simulated environment; allow skip. Preserve pause, audio settings, radar, fullscreen, HUD modes and old/new dragon toggle. No destructive scenario in real life; all mechanics are fictional.

### Campaign contract (DD-002/003/022)
15 waves; verified source test defines chapter boundaries after 2,5,10,14 and assisted finale with Accountability Victory / Escape Ending, defeat, replay. Integration tutorial source defines flight, fire, buster, fire suppression, nonlethal gas and graduation; Tab/button skip. Preserve evacuation/escort objectives, orbital finale, chapter cleanup and cutscene state. Earlier requested news helicopters after 4, police/fire after 7, police helicopters after 8, day-to-night at 6; integration source has wave>=6 ICE/blockade and wave>=7 hostile emergency behavior. Do not overwrite either timeline by inference: reconcile a single approved spawn table, including enemy roles, day/night, escalation, powerups, scoring and endings. Large scripted opening spectacle historically requested; gentler tutorial/pacing also requested. Tune based on actual novice play and seed-locked metrics, not automation that idles Paulo.
 
### Controls, threats and physics (DD-004–010)
Source action module binds WASD/arrows, Space fire, F gas, P pause, F3 debug; actual pointer bindings and wind hold live in game entry. Historical short-left-click fire, long press wind; verify exact hold duration and right-button behavior per build. Aim ray and hit effects share world coordinates; first object along ray receives breath. Distinct foreground targetable and distant non-targetable meteors; no invisible immunity at playable depth. Spawn offscreen, clear warning and radar, preserve fragments and intercept scoring. Small meteors one hit; size-based split hierarchy, giant finale split, harmless dust. Fast meteors can bump Paulo. Wind must push heavy vehicles, tip cars, displace pedestrians/debris, deflect rocks/aircraft and put out fires. Gas nonlethal 7s responder freeze; lingering cloud falls/lingers, cannot extend same responder indefinitely. Distinguish player meteor interceptions from military interceptions for scoring. Ground turn rears up; flight banks with tucked feet; old/new model comparison. No animation choice can replace a working input or collision contract.

### Destruction (DD-011–015)
Maintain separate school HP, per-parcel damage, neighborhood health, and distant skyline state. First swept 3D collision point determines ownership; do not destroy neighboring houses by direct damage when one ordinary meteor hits a roof. Shock/debris collateral is a separately specified event, with radius, cause and attribution; do not let a special colossal event silently waive ordinary-hit isolation. School small 5%, large 15% ordinary reference strikes; gradual local glass/facade/structural penetration and persistent rubble. School at zero is leveled, not floating; repair/restart clears strikes, fires, fragments, debris, timers, colliders and inspector state. Fire anchored to actual impacted windows/roofs and causal meteor-only structural ignition baseline (other weapon/vehicle impacts and electrical sparks may have distinct approved effects). Historical fire drain target 1–2 percentage points/sec: aggregate vs per-fire and cap unresolved. Skyline distant simulation can be cheaper but must visibly respond to its own impacts and disaster progression.

### Architecture (DD-016–020)
Source school is the anchor; game camera faces east, screen-left/negative x north toward Dauphin, right/positive x south toward Susquehanna (RC59 reference; validate transformed integration coordinate system). School FBX remains byte-for-byte immutable; derived play mesh is not original. Interior accuracy mandatory for school; other interiors approximate but functionally plausible (church/auditorium open assembly space, retail, residential, restaurant, dorm). No generic floor slabs crossing nave. Preserve real street footprints, heights, frontage setbacks, clear roads/sidewalks, parking and north/south flight range. Replace legacy geometry, not overlay. Architecture inventory uses stable IDs, source date, footprint/height, model and material hashes, reference/evidence confidence, LOD, damage ownership, provisional flags and completion rubric. Reference images are not textures to redistribute. Do not invent precise survey dimensions. Compare capture dates, newer trustworthy evidence wins.
 
Scope: Broad Street corridor toward 13th Street; distant Park–13th detail may be lower. Reusable rowhouse template pilot 2428–2432 N Park: fit each footprint/height and customize evidence-backed windows, doors, porches, roof, steps, brick, railings, awnings. Mark flipped/mixed Watts rears used as temporary opposite Park proxies; do not certify as real facades. Protect church stained glass, McDonald's flat parapet (current reference), pharmacy parking lot, AutoZone signage, dorms, southern blocks and school adjacency; capture front/side/rear/roof views. All decorative elements physically touch their intended supports; glass transparency/reflection, interior light alignment; STL watertight/non-manifold validation is separate and deferred. Maintain record-level completion rubric: massing 20, front 25, side/rear 15, roof 10, materials 15, QA 15 (existing tracker). Scores require evidence and cannot mean release certification. Integration 10 uses 122 damageable mapped parcels; Integration 11 introduces 165 opt-in background records without replacing those 122. Do not conflate 123 model records in older architectural tracker (school included) with 122 gameplay parcels. Expansion evidence remains incomplete; held parcels must not be invented. 

### Visual and performance (DD-021–025)
Road traffic two lanes each way; trucks/buses/cars/emergency vehicles, pedestrians, rotor orientation correct, collision/wind/braking and avoidance. Causal electricity, water-main, subway and structural effects independently testable. Real Philadelphia skyline (Liberty, Comcast, City Hall) with credible scale and distance; day/dusk/night lights align to glass. Smoke varied, flame geometry anchored, no oversized detached torches; no timer-generated fires to fake impact. Compare actual game frames to campaign/visual_target.png, not AI artwork. RC59 art remains failed according to its handoff; no extrapolation to Integration 10/11. Keep fixed timestep, bounded work and frame governor; pooled particles/combustion, stable lights, batch static BIM/vehicle meshes, LOD and shader/pixel limits. Reject input stalls, runaway resources or changes that lower detail without explicit evidence. Native GPU timing separate from headless software WebGL. Hardware target RTX 3060 for later Unity; current Preview 11 gate is explicit and scoped.

## Portability and asset contract
Keep engine-neutral data and collision/damage fixtures; separate simulation time from presentation. Preserve units, origin, handedness, world-to-parcel transform and exact source provenance. Store high-quality masters with material channels (base color, normal, roughness, metalness, opacity), mesh IDs, transforms, hierarchy, rig/animations, modular destruction component IDs and separate LOD derivatives. Convert once per hash; do not overwrite FBX. Unity HDRP import later: validate axis/scale, materials, colliders, animation retarget, deterministic fixtures and native GPU. No porting as a browser release prerequisite.
