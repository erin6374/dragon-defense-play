Model specification

Original realistic-mascot hybrid. Charcoal scale plates, crimson wing membrane, bronze horns, amber eyes. A four-legged, two-winged silhouette; head and neck counterbalance a segmented tail.

Wing rig: shoulder → elbow → wrist → outer fingers. Wingbeats are asymmetric in banks, with fold and span changes for glide and takeoff. Chest, jaw, neck and tail are separately articulated.

Animation states

Prototype limits

This is a low-poly rig and timing test, not the final sculpt or skinned mesh. It deliberately reuses geometry and materials. Fire, wind and gas use pooled visual-effect previews attached to the animated head; they do not change gameplay, weather, collisions or fire suppression.
