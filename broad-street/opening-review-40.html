How to interpret the counts

A window is a principal architectural opening. A bay with several panes counts as one bay; glass mullions are not extra windows. Counts represent the model's major street-facing openings, and are tagged photo-visible, photo-partial, or estimated. Unknown door counts are shown as —, rather than fabricated. Exact dimensions and hidden street-level openings still require verification.

Important limitations

Do not infer the number of doors from a blocked storefront shutter or an architectural bay's individual glass panels. The saved municipal footprints determine massing and frontage. Source photos are retained in your existing ChatGPT Library and identified by filename; Google Street View image copies have not been bundled into this package.
